A faithful document-conversion workflow

Instructor work, made accessible—without changing what it says.

Lumos Translator helps an AI assistant turn instructor question and answer materials into accessible Word and PDF documents without silently losing questions, solution steps, figures, or annotations.

Use Lumos in an AI assistant View canonical workflow

Designed for AI assistants with web and file capabilities. Available formats and DOCX/PDF creation depend on the AI you use.

The simple route

  1. 01 Open an AI assistant with web and file capabilities.
  2. 02 Say: “Use Lumos Translator.”
  3. 03 Provide your question and answer source sets.
  4. 04 Receive Word and PDF when the host supports them.

Two source sets

Give Lumos the structure and the instructor’s original answer material.

You do not need to merge, rename, or pre-sort files. Each set may contain several files.

Required

Question Source Set

What students see: the assignment, exam, worksheet, quiz, or other question material.

Required

Answer Source Set

The instructor’s answers, worked solutions, rubric, annotations, diagrams, or explanations.

Sources can be documents, scans, screenshots, photos, audio, video, or other formats the AI you choose can actually read.

What Lumos protects

Reconstruction, not reinvention.

  • Preserves instructor-authored academic content and method.
  • Maps answer fragments by meaning, not handwriting position alone.
  • Checks for omissions, duplication, and misplaced material.
  • Does not independently solve, grade, or “fix” an instructor’s answer.
  • Does not add missing reasoning or require routine manual cleanup.

Intended outputs

Editable for revision. Readable for distribution.

Word / DOCXPDF

Lumos Translator is a public, web-discoverable workflow—not a native AI plugin.
